Behavior summary

  • Position: bottom-right, 16 px above taskbar, 16 px from screen edge. Draggable later via tray-menu “Move HUD”.
  • Always-on-top: yes — but stays visible only when non-fullscreen apps are focused. Auto-hides during fullscreen video/games.
  • Click-through: OFF by default. Mouse clicks on the HUD do HUD things (right-click for menu, double-click opens dashboard).
  • Refresh cadence: every 5 seconds (same as the tracker tick).
  • Bar colors: cyan under limit, amber at 75–99%, red at ≥100%.
  • Deep-work footer: only shown when a session is active.
  • Tray menu adds: “Show HUD” / “Hide HUD” toggle, persisted across launches.
